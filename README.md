# 🇸🇦 KSA Helper

### Know what to do next.

KSA Helper is a simple bilingual web application designed to help people living in Saudi Arabia understand where to go and what to do when dealing with common everyday government and administrative services.

Instead of trying to figure out whether you need Absher, Qiwa, Najiz, Balady, Musaned, or another platform, KSA Helper helps you start with your **problem** and points you toward the appropriate official service.

> **Tell us your problem. Know what to do next.**

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Saudi Arabia provides many government services digitally through platforms such as Absher, Qiwa, Najiz, Balady, Musaned, and others.

The challenge for many people isn't necessarily accessing these services.

It's knowing **where to start**.

For example:

* Where do I check my Iqama?
* What should I do if my Iqama is expiring?
* Where can I find my employment contract?
* Which platform handles Exit/Re-entry?
* Where do I go for a salary-related issue?
* Which platform handles court services?
* Where can I access municipal services?
* How do I calculate my end-of-service benefit?

KSA Helper aims to make finding the answer easier.

---

## 💡 The Solution

KSA Helper provides a simple interface where users can search for their situation or select a common problem.

For example:

```text
My Iqama is expiring
```

```text
I need help with my employment contract
```

```text
I have a salary issue
```

```text
I need Exit/Re-entry help
```

KSA Helper then provides simple next steps and directs the user to the relevant official Saudi service.

The goal is not to replace government platforms.

The goal is to help users understand **which platform they need and what they should do next.**

# 📅 Expiry Tracker

KSA Helper includes a simple expiry-date tracker.

* Iqama
* Passport
* Driver's licence
* Insurance
* Vehicle registration
* Employment contract

KSA Helper then shows how many days remain before the selected item expires.

---

# 🔐 Privacy

KSA Helper is designed with privacy in mind.

The current application does not require users to create an account.

Expiry dates are stored locally within the user's browser and are not sent to a KSA Helper server.

KSA Helper should never ask users to provide sensitive information such as:

* Iqama numbers
* Passport numbers
* Passwords
* OTP verification codes
* Banking credentials
* Government account passwords

# 🗺️ What's Next?

KSA Helper is currently an early MVP.

Future improvements may include:

### 🧭 Guided Problem Solving

Instead of simply showing information, KSA Helper will ask users a few questions about their situation.

For example:

```text
My Iqama has expired
        ↓
Are you currently employed?
        ↓
Is your employment contract active?
        ↓
Has your employer started the renewal process?
        ↓
Recommended next steps
```

This will allow KSA Helper to provide more relevant guidance.

Improve search so users can describe problems naturally rather than knowing the exact name of a service.

Expand the platform to cover more everyday situations experienced by people living in Saudi Arabia.

Potential future languages include:

* Urdu
* Hindi
* Bengali
* Tagalog

### 📱 Mobile Experience

Continue improving KSA Helper for mobile users and explore making the application installable as a Progressive Web App.

Explore optional reminders for important expiry dates without requiring users to provide sensitive identification information.
